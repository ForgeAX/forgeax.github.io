One large preview build. A project can now own its loading and failure screens, WebGL2 gains terrain, morphs, decals, soft shadows, MSAA and temporal anti-aliasing, a many-lights test scene went from 39 to 60 frames per second, and DevKit starts faster when little has changed.

Engine 0.6 preview: your own loading screens, far more rendering on WebGL2, faster warm starts

  • Preview build 0.6.0-dev.16818, covering three days of work.
  • Your own loading and failure screens: set devkitAppOptions.startupUi to supply your own HTML, CSS and script for each screen, or turn a screen off. The same screens are used in development, browser builds and WeChat Mini Games, and the built-in screen now shows your game title.
  • Far more rendering on WebGL2: morph surfaces, terrain layers, contact shadows and decals, physical-sky image-based lighting and soft shadows, plus MSAA, batched instance LOD, capsule shadows, and TAA, TAAU and TSR temporal anti-aliasing and upscaling.
  • Temporal super resolution at native resolution is available as an opt-in for both the Forward and Deferred paths. Defaults are unchanged.
  • Sturdier on more devices: a lost WebGL context now recovers, compressed textures are qualified on mobile, and WebGPU modes on Ubuntu are qualified with a real renderer fallback.
  • Faster scenes with many lights: a test scene lit by many spot lights went from 39 to 60 frames per second at 1080p, through vertex-only shadow pipelines, steadier shadow updates and tighter light culling. The same work fixed lights going missing on very large triangles.
  • Faster iteration: DevKit warm starts reuse prepared assets and restore unchanged ones from cache, and forgeax project check now dry-cooks material and particle packs offline, so mistakes surface before you run the game.
  • Effects, image quality and import: effects can read their effect player's time and world time and declare typed parameters you override per effect player, specular anti-aliasing, particles drawn before temporal anti-aliasing, screen-space reflections on planar receivers, generated tangents that follow glTF handedness, and reliable import and packing for projects with many glTF files.
