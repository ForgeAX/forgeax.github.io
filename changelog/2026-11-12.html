Engine stable v0.5.0: two weeks of preview work reaches stable, bloom recalibrated

  • SDK v0.5.0 is the new stable release, two weeks after v0.4.19, published on GitHub Releases as a self-contained archive with provenance, an SPDX software bill of materials and SHA-256 checksums. Three preview builds, 0.6.0-dev.16819 through 0.6.0-dev.16821, shipped in the same two days.
  • New in stable since v0.4.19: several directional lights in one scene, more glTF extensions, video textures, the mesh modeling tools, automatic choice of rendering path by device, far wider WebGL2 rendering including temporal anti-aliasing and upscaling, your own loading and failure screens, and faster warm starts.
  • Breaking change, bloom: bloomIntensity is now the share of bright light scattered as glow, so a scene tuned earlier shows about a third of its previous glow; multiply the old value by 3 to keep the look. bloomThreshold now defaults to -1, meaning no threshold, so coloured emissive surfaces glow; set it explicitly to keep a cut-off.
  • Breaking change, lens and reflections: chromatic aberration is now a radial fringe that leaves the centre untouched and grows toward the corners. chromaticAberration is a percentage from 0 to 5, chromaticAberrationAngle is removed and chromaticAberrationStartOffset is added. PlanarReflection.distortion is now a world-space radius instead of a screen offset.
  • Sky and fog: the full physical atmosphere runs on the WebGL2 Forward path, fog now covers the sky background with Fog.skyOpacity to control it, the sky follows the sun's brightness and tint, and there is a new ADDITIVE_BLEND material preset, a setTransformLookAt helper and a filmic tone-mapping mode.
  • Video and colour fixes: a video used as an ordinary texture is no longer upside down in browsers without zero-copy video import, and an identity colour lookup table now leaves the picture unchanged instead of darkening shadows.
  • Performance: a depth prepass on the Forward path, physics that reports motion changes without polling resting bodies, frames credited as soon as their own GPU work completes, and, in Android and iOS browsers, a drawing buffer that defaults to 720 pixels on its short side.
  • In the preview builds: forgeax project play --release runs your scripted playthrough against the built release, --session keeps a warm browser between runs, a later step can start from a setup step instead of replaying the whole game, and mouse.look turns a mouse-look camera. The 3D starter is now split into player, camera, world and UI folders that can each be deleted.
