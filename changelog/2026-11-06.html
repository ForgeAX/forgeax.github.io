Engine 0.6 preview: automatic rendering path, WebGL2 catches up, tools that show what the renderer sees

Two preview builds this week. The Standard pipeline now picks its rendering path from what the device can do, WebGL2 gains shadows, sprites, instance LOD and a physical sky, and two new DevKit commands let you and your AI agent see scene brightness and identify the surface under any pixel.

Engine 0.6 preview: automatic rendering path, WebGL2 catches up, tools that show what the renderer sees

  • Two preview builds, 0.6.0-dev.16816 and 0.6.0-dev.16817.
  • Automatic rendering path: the Standard pipeline now chooses between its Forward and Deferred paths from the device's capabilities, so one project runs across a wider range of devices without per-device settings.
  • WebGL2 catches up: shadow receiving, sprite paging, instance LOD, qualified Standard effects and ambient occlusion, plus a portable physical sky background.
  • See what the renderer sees: dev look --view luminance writes a false-colour heat map of scene brightness in stops around the bloom threshold, with a JSON summary, and dev pick --x --y reports the surface under a pixel: its entity, mesh, material slot and world position. Both are built so an AI agent can diagnose a glowing or wrong-looking pixel instead of guessing.
  • Planar reflections: PlanarReflection.resolutionScale sets the reflection resolution and generateMipmaps blurs the reflection by surface roughness.
  • GPU capture inspection got faster: a per-pass summary, and pipelines and bindings can be inspected without replaying the capture.
  • Character controllers can be reconciled to an authoritative position without repeated pushes, and character movement respects input time. The canvas pixel ratio now defaults to 1 for predictable performance; opt into device density with canvasPixelRatio.
  • Also in these builds: portable Hi-Z, screen-space reflection and histogram exposure plans, cheaper changes to instance counts, a textured vehicle in the 3D starter built from assembly constraints, and clearer template documentation for the dev and build flow.
